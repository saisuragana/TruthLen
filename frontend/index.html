02 / WHAT WE LOOK AT

A few focused checks

✦

Photos

Looks for visual patterns a model associates with AI-made pictures.

Videos

Checks a handful of frames and, when possible, the sound track. It does not study motion between frames.

Voice recordings

Checks audio for signs associated with generated or altered speech. Music and noise can confuse this check.

i

TruthLen uses existing models. It has not been trained or calibrated on a large TruthLen test collection.
